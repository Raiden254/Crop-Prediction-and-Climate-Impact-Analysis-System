Crop Yield Prediction & Climate Impact Analysis System

Welcome to the Crop Yield Prediction and Climate Impact Analysis dashboard. This platform provides insights into how various climatic factors influence crop yields, helping farmers and stakeholders make informed decisions.

About This System

This system utilizes machine learning models to predict crop yields based on historical data and climate variables. It also analyzes how climate factors such as rainfall and temperature influence crop yield hence offering valuable information for sustainable farming practices.

Key Features

  • Crop Yield Prediction: Predict future crop yields based on historical data and climate variables.
  • Climate Impact Analysis: Analyze how different climatic factors affect crop yields.
  • Interactive Visualizations: Explore data through interactive charts and graphs.
  • Data-Driven Insights: Gain insights to optimize farming practices and improve crop management.

How it works

  1. Climate data (rainfall, temperature) is analyzed.
  2. Crop yield trends are visualized.
  3. Machine learning models are trained on historical data.
  4. User inputs are used to generate yield predictions.

Data Sources

The data used in this project is sourced from the Kenya National Bureau of Statistics via open source Africa and Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations. Meteorological data from Kenya Weather Station Data (2015–2025) via Kaggle, ensuring accuracy and reliability for analysis and predictions.

How to Use This Dashboard

Navigate through the sidebar to explore different crops and their relationships with climate factors. Use the interactive charts to analyze trends and make data-driven decisions for better crop management.

  • Climate Analysis: Explore environmental trends.
  • Crop Analysis: Dive into specific crop performance.
  • Yield Predictions: Get forecasts based on current data.
